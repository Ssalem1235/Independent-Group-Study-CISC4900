Environmental Impacts of AI

Global Data Center Electricity Consumption (2020–2030)

Global data center electricity consumption grew from 270 TWh in 2020 to 415 TWh in 2024, a 54% increase in just four years. AI's specific share of that consumption grew even faster, rising from 30 TWh to 130 TWh over the same period, more than quadrupling. If IEA projections hold, total data center consumption will reach 945 TWh by 2030, with AI workloads accounting for the majority of that demand.
